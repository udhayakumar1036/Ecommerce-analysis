
## 1. Increase Repeat Purchases
A significant number of customers are one-time buyers. The business should use targeted discounts, email campaigns, and personalized product recommendations to encourage a second purchase.

## 2. Retain High-Value Customers
Champions and Loyal Customers generate significant value. The business should provide loyalty rewards, exclusive offers, and personalized promotions to retain these customers.

## 3. Re-engage At-Risk Customers
At-risk customers should receive win-back campaigns, special discounts, and personalized communication to encourage them to return.

## 4. Focus on High-Performing Products
Products such as Paper Craft and Little Birdie show strong sales performance. The business should maintain sufficient inventory and promote these products.

## 5. Strengthen High-Performing Markets
The United Kingdom generates the highest revenue. The business should maintain strong operations in this market while exploring growth opportunities in other high-performing countries.

## 6. Plan Around Sales Trends
The business should use monthly sales trends to plan inventory, marketing campaigns, and promotions during high-demand periods.
